About the Film

The Approach

Before I graduated high school I worked on a found footage film with a bunch of my friends. Staring my friend Sean, the story centered on him finding a camcorder in the woods before discovering an abandoned bunker with weird noises echoing from within. For all of our begaining shots we used vintage Soviet lenses to capture a warm moody feel, and this added a lot more depth to the film.

My Role

This was a great learning experience from all angles and let me see every intricate aspect of making a movie. As the director I had the great oppertunity to work with my team in ways I have never done before. Having our friend Sean as the wanderer in the found footage was a great choice because he was able to adapt quickly when we needed to change the script. We learned many things, from camera framing, lighting, and even avoiding conflict when wanting to kill each other after the twentieth take. Although the short film was never finished that summer, the experience was well worth the effort.

We still plan on releasing the film once shooting finishes this next summer and it will be avalible to the public on YouTube.
