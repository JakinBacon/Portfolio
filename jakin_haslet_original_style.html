ABOUT

David is a Cinematographer / director of photography based in London & the UK.

"I want to bring creativity, persistence, and a team-first attitude to every film project I take on.

 I believe that the best cinematography goes unnoticed. I see my role as drawing together all the visual elements in front of my lens to deliver the directors intention & vision as their collaborator.

 In an increasingly noisy world it is our art that can reach the hearts of people. My hope is that I can make a difference though my camera."
