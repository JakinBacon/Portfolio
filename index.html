About

I'm Jakin, a film production student drawn to visual storytelling, atmosphere, and stories that connect with people.

During the pandemic, I took a dive into the world of Lego stop motion, which eventually led to bigger projects and short films. Taking inspiration from Wallace and Gromit and Chicken Run I set off to make my own films. Animation requires an insane amount of dedication and focus. Each individual movement needs to be precise and intentional, so during this time I learned a lot about what it means to keep focus under pressure.

Purpose

“Just like the Bible shows the greatest story of all time, I want to show the world how God is still moving through my stories. ”

I value teamwork, creativity, and persistence. You cannot make a film without a crew, much less one who does their very best and is driven to innovate. I am motivated by the purpose God has given me to glorify Him through media and creativity, as well as to connect with people who care about his great design. It is through this purpose that I can circle back and accomplish the dream that a young me would have.
